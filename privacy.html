Privacy policy

What the app accesses

Mac Mini Backup uses Google authorization to access Drive files and metadata needed for personal backup jobs. Its Frigate integration requests the drive.file scope to create and manage files belonging to that integration. Google account credentials are entered directly into Google's sign-in service.

How data is used

The app copies selected local files into the owner's Google Drive account, checks uploaded copies, and deletes backups according to the locally configured retention policy. It stores authorization tokens, backup manifests, checksums and operational logs on the owner's computer.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored on the owner's computers and in Google Drive. Google processes those files under its own service terms and privacy policy. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. The public documentation site does not receive backup files or authorization tokens.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the owner's backup-job settings. The Frigate integration is configured for seven-day cloud retention; outages can delay deletion. The owner can remove backup files in Google Drive and delete local state. Revoking authorization stops future API access but does not itself erase existing backup files.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke the app's access through Google Account connections. Questions can be directed to the maintainer, Sean Pianka.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
