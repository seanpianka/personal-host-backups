PERSONAL HOST BACKUPS

Mac Mini Backup

A personal backup utility maintained by Sean Pianka. It uses rclone to copy selected files from the owner's computers to the owner's Google Drive account, verify those copies, and remove older backups according to the configured retention policy.

Purpose and access

This app is for the owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public backup service. Google authorization permits the configured backup jobs to access Drive files within their granted scopes.

The Frigate backup integration requests drive.file access to create and manage its own backup files. Retention and local cleanup are controlled by the owner's local configuration.

Data practices

Read the privacy policy for details about Google data access, storage and deletion, and the terms of use for the app's operating limits.
